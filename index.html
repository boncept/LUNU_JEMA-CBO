01 · WHO WE ARE

Community begins with wellbeing.

Lunu Jema CBO is a community-based organization working in Lamu Island and Mokowe to build healthier, more empowered, and resilient communities.

Our work brings together community knowledge, health education, nutrition, youth and women’s empowerment, environmental responsibility, agriculture and food security, and long-term community development.
